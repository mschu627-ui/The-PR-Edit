## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the calculated elegance of high-fashion print publishing merged with the analytical precision of artificial intelligence. Designed for luxury fashion directors, PR strategists, and boutique fashion houses, the aesthetic evokes the quiet authority of *Vogue Business*, *Porter*, and *Kinfolk*. It trades digital clutter for generous negative space, immaculate typographic scale, and delicate structural framing.

The visual style embraces an elevated Luxury Editorial Minimalism. The atmosphere feels curated and contemplative rather than transactional, signaling deep strategic value rather than ephemeral hype. Visual hierarchy relies on hairline dividers, museum-grade tonal pacing, and deliberate typographic tension: literary, high-contrast serif headlines paired against pragmatic, micro-tracked sans-serif metrics and labels. The emotional resonance is discreet, discerning, and razor-sharp.

## Colors

The palette draws from natural parchment, archival ink, and tailored textiles.

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas: Warm cream (`#F9F5ED`), creating an organic, gallery-like reading backdrop.
  - Primary cards & elevated sheets: Crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) to spotlight analytical reports, dossiers, and curated clippings.
  - Inset containers & nested tiers: Linen parchment (`#F4EFE6`), used to isolate metadata sidebars, filter wells, and secondary panels.

- **Typography & Structural Accents:**
  - Primary text & headlines: Slate charcoal (`#4A5258`), delivering refined legibility without the stark harshness of synthetic pure black.
  - Secondary metadata & captions: Muted charcoal (`#4A5560`), providing balanced visual weight for dense contextual intelligence.
  - Structural hairlines & borders: Cool slate wash (`#E0E6EA`) and soft celadon mist (`#D5DDD0`) applied strictly at 1px thickness.

- **Editorial Accents & Intelligence Status:**
  - Secondary Spindle blue (`#8A99A8`) and pale wash (`#E0E6EA`): Applied to analytical trend graphs, AI narrative tags, and archival pill states.
  - Warm stone gray (`#D3CDC3`): Reserved strictly for high-relevance editorial picks, key VIP alert badges, and AI intelligence synthesis indicators.
  - Semantic Status:
    - *Engage*: Deep laurel leaf (`#3E5A44`) on a muted linen wash.
    - *Monitor*: Spindle slate (`#4A5560`) with an airy grey pill container.
    - *Skip / Dismiss*: Desert rosewood (`#8C5353`) kept minimal, avoiding jarring digital alert states.

## Typography

Typography functions as the primary visual anchor of this design system, creating an immediate dialogue between classic editorial mastheads and hyper-modern analytical reporting.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set exclusively in *Playfair Display*. Used with generous vertical line spacing to simulate the typesetting of print editorial features. It expresses sophistication, storytelling, and curatorial intellect.
- **Body & Contextual Copy:** Set in *Inter*. Tuned for effortless readability when parsing dense PR intelligence briefs, media outreach logs, and journalist profile backgrounds.
- **Labels, System Metadata & Indicators:** Set in *Inter* uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.06em` to `0.12em`) at smaller point sizes (`10px`–`12px`). This establishes an authoritative technical counterpoint to the romantic warmth of the serif headlines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors magazine page architecture with a structural fluid grid system.

- **Desktop & Wide Displays (1200px+):** A 12-column layout with 24px gutters and generous outer margins (48px to 64px) to preserve high visual aeration. Content should rarely exceed 1400px centered max-width, maintaining an intimate reading measure.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** An 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px margins. Secondary context sidebars collapse into toggleable off-canvas drawers or stacked tabs.
- **Mobile (~390px Viewport):** A 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 20px outer margins. Vertical pacing compresses smoothly using `space-md` (16px) for item separation while maintaining wide breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) between distinct thematic sections.

Layout structure prioritizes strict vertical alignment along thin architectural border lines. Sections avoid edge-to-edge full bleed cards, preferring inset cards framed by cream margins.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system intentionally rejects heavy, synthetic drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Architectural Hairlines**.

- **Z-Index Layering:** Depth is achieved by placing pure white `#FFFFFF` cards atop the `#F9F5ED` warm cream canvas, occasionally grounded with `#F4EFE6` for tertiary insets.
- **Hairline Framing:** Visual separation relies on crisp, 1px solid outlines in `#E0E6EA` and `#D5DDD0`.
- **Soft Editorial Shadow:** When floating modals, contextual dropdowns, or mobile sticky navigation bars require separation from scrolling feeds, use a diffused, ambient, warm-tinted shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(74, 82, 88, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(74, 82, 88, 0.03);`
- **Glass Tinting:** Modals and sticky headers utilize an ultra-subtle frosted glass backing (`rgba(249, 245, 237, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) edged by a 1px baseline border to preserve clarity.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural corner language. A roundedness factor of `1` (Soft) is employed for foundational structural surfaces to evoke crisp fine-art matting and tailored card stock.

- **Cards, Panels & Sheet Overlays:** Standard corner radius of `4px` (`0.25rem`). Large modal dialogs and curated imagery containers may utilize `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** Status indicators (e.g., *Engage*, *Monitor*, *Skip*) and relevance scores break the rectilinear grid by utilizing full capsule pill radii (`9999px`), ensuring immediate recognition as interactive and qualitative data points.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Slate Charcoal (`#4A5258`) background with Warm Cream (`#F9F5ED`) text. Rectangular with a delicate 4px radius, padded with `12px 24px`. Micro-tracked uppercase label (`label-lg`), devoid of gradient or heavy shadow.
- **Secondary / Editorial Action:** Transparent background with a 1px hairline border (`#4A5258`), Slate Charcoal text, subtly shifting to `#F4EFE6` on hover.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Text-only in `#4A5258` with a persistent or hover-activated 1px underline positioned 4px beneath the baseline.

### Chips & Status Indicators
- **PR Action Pills (*Engage / Monitor / Skip*):**
  - Fully rounded capsules (`padding: 4px 10px`).
  - *Engage*: Soft muted sage background (`rgba(62, 90, 68, 0.08)`) with `#2F4533` text and a subtle 1px border.
  - *Monitor*: Soft blue-grey background (`rgba(138, 153, 168, 0.15)`) with `#3B464F` text.
  - *Skip*: Muted taupe wash with `#786C68` text.
- **Relevance / Match Score:** Minimal circular badge or pill containing a single numerical value (e.g., `96% Match`) styled with a warm stone hairline indicator (`#D3CDC3`).

### Intelligence & Feed Cards
- Background is crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) bound by a 1px `#E0E6EA` outline.
- Padding follows an asymmetrical editorial hierarchy: spacious top and bottom (`space-lg`), balanced side margins.
- Features high-contrast serif article headlines, tracked sans metadata (outlet, journalist, beat, reach), and an embedded action tray pinned to the card bottom.

### Form Inputs & Filters
- **Text Inputs:** Framed by a 1px border in `#D5DDD0` against an off-white `#FFFFFF` or `#F4EFE6` fill. Under focus, the border shifts to `#4A5258` with no outer glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimal, square 16px boxes with hairline borders. Checked state fills with solid `#4A5258` containing an off-center geometric checkmark or interior square.

### Navigation (Desktop & Mobile)
- **Desktop:** Minimal top masthead with an editorial logo anchor, clean inline category links, search prompt, and brand switcher.
- **Mobile (~390px Viewport):**
  - Sticky glass header with brand masthead and an off-canvas drawer trigger for navigation.
  - Fixed bottom contextual navigation bar (`height: 60px`) featuring hairline top border (`#E0E6EA`), glass backing, and iconic micro-labels for *Intelligence Feed*, *Pitch Planner*, *Media Book*, and *Saved Dossiers*.
